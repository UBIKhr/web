During September and October, the TROPHY project carried out a series of important activities, among which the Summer School “E-Business Technologies 2025” was one of the highlights.

The Summer School was hosted online by the University of Belgrade, Faculty of Organizational Sciences, from 3 to 19 September, with final project presentations taking place on 27 September.

The program provided students and early-stage innovators with hands-on experience in Web 4.0-based digital product development, generative artificial intelligence, and blockchain applications. Through a combination of lectures, practical workshops, and mentored project sprints, participants developed functional prototypes while acquiring knowledge and skills applicable in innovation and entrepreneurship settings.

This activity reflects the mission of the TROPHY project, to which UBIK actively contributes as a partner, focused on empowering higher education institutions, academic staff, and students through the development of digital and entrepreneurial competencies, as well as strengthening collaboration between academia and industry.
