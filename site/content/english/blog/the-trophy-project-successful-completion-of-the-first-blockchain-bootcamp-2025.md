In July, the TROPHY project successfully delivered the first edition of the Blockchain Bootcamp 2025, providing students with hands-on experience in Solidity, smart contracts, and decentralized applications.

Participants engaged with online learning materials and practical projects, developing functional solutions and earning certificates of completion. The program focused on building skills directly applicable to innovation and entrepreneurship in the field of blockchain technologies.

Since early November, the second, self-paced edition of the Bootcamp has been underway. This iteration enables participants to explore Web3 technologies at their own pace through seven comprehensive modules and more than 20 hours of recorded lectures.

The program covers key areas:

* smart contract development
* decentralized applications (DApps)
* tokenization
* blockchain-based business models

As a project partner, UBIK contributes to activities aimed at strengthening digital and entrepreneurial competencies while promoting the adoption of advanced technologies within the broader community.
