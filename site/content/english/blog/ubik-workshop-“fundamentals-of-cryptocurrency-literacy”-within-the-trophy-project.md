UBIK invites you to a free educational workshop “Fundamentals of Cryptocurrency Literacy”, organized as part of the TROPHY project.

Location: Faculty of Economics in Osijek
Date: December 12, 2025 (16:00-21:00) & December 13, 2025 (11:00-16:00)

What to expect?
This free workshop is designed for anyone who wants to understand the fundamental concepts of blockchain and cryptocurrencies and apply them in practice. Through interactive lectures and practical examples, participants will learn:

* basic concepts of cryptography and blockchain technology
* how to use cryptocurrencies in practice
* how to recognize risks, common online scams, and security challenges
* how to participate in the cryptocurrency market responsibly and in an informed manner

The number of seats is limited-secure your spot on time.
