The Deep Tech Intro Program 2025, organized by the University of Belgrade, Faculty of Organizational Sciences, and Centre for Research & Technology Hellas (CERTH) within the EIT HEI project TROPHY, is currently in progress and approaching its final phase.

Designed for academic staff and students, the program offers a structured introduction to deep-tech domains including artificial intelligence, machine learning, digital twins, design thinking, and innovation contexts related to Web3 technologies. It combines live online lectures, hybrid workshops, and self-paced learning.

Lecturers and contributors include:

* Prof. Dr. Zorica Bogdanovic (FON) - deep tech fundamentals
* Dr. Ana Milicevic (FON) - design thinking workshop
* Prof. Dr. Sasa Milic (Electrical Engineering Institute “Nikola Tesla”) - neural networks, self-paced module
* Nikos Tsalikidis (CERTH) - digital twins and energy-related applications
* Aggeliki Veliskaki (CERTH) - digital twin platforms and case studies
* Dr. Aristeidis Mystakidis (CERTH) - machine learning integration and deployment
* Dr. Marija Jankovic (CERTH) - AI Act compliance and trustworthy AI systems

All lectures, recordings, and materials are available via Moodle, allowing participants to follow the program at their own pace.

The program continues over the coming days with expert-led sessions focused on real-world deployment, system integration, and regulatory considerations in deep-tech innovation.
