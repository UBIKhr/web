We are proud to share that the EIT Higher Education Initiative has awarded funding to the TROPHY - The Next Generation of Deep Technology Leaders project as part of the 2025 Call for Proposals (Strand B).

As a project partner, UBIK - Croatian Blockchain and Cryptocurrency Association is excited to contribute to an initiative aimed at transforming higher education institutions into drivers of Web 4.0 innovation. TROPHY focuses on equipping students with advanced technical and entrepreneurial skills while strengthening collaboration between academia and industry.

By emphasizing deep tech leadership and interdisciplinary learning, the project will enhance innovation capacity across Europe and empower the next generation of change-makers.

The project is coordinated by the University of Belgrade, Faculty of Organizational Sciences, and brings together a distinguished international consortium of partners from Serbia, Croatia, Portugal, Spain, and Greece.

Through this collaboration, TROPHY contributes to building resilient, future-ready educational ecosystems and leadership models tailored for the Web 4.0 era.

We are proud to be part of the EIT HEI community and look forward to shaping a more innovative, tech-driven, and collaborative future.
